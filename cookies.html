Storage & Transparency

Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Service Provider: ChoreMatrix

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 local storage are standard browser technologies that allow web applications to remember your preferences and session state between page visits.

2. How ChoreMatrix Uses Storage

1. Strictly Necessary Storage Required

Essential for core functionality. Without this storage, you cannot remain logged in or use saved households.

  • chorematrix_auth_token: Stores your Supabase authentication session token.
  • chorematrix_theme: Remembers your selected theme (Light, Dark, or System).
  • chorematrix_cookie_consent: Remembers your privacy consent choices.
2. UI Preference Storage Optional

Remembers UI layout choices such as Calendar vs. Table view and the active week tab. You can disable this category in cookie preferences.

3. Analytics & Telemetry Disabled

Product usage analytics are currently disabled. ChoreMatrix does not install Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or third-party behavioral telemetry.

4. Advertising & Cross-Site Tracking None

ChoreMatrix contains zero advertising trackers and does not sell or share data with ad exchanges.

3. Managing Your Choices

You can adjust your consent choices at any time without penalty. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
