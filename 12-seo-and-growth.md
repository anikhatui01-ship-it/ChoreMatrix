# 12 — SEO & Growth Strategy

## Brand

ChoreMatrix

## Primary positioning

**Free Household Chore Planner & Workload Optimizer**

## Search themes

Primary:

- chore chart
- chore planner
- household chore planner
- chore schedule
- chore schedule generator

Secondary:

- family chore chart
- family chore planner
- roommate chore chart
- couple chore chart
- fair chore schedule
- household workload calculator
- chore assignment generator

## Landing page strategy

Use real tools, not thin SEO pages.

### Initial pages

- `/`
- `/chore-planner`
- `/chore-chart`
- `/chore-schedule-generator`
- `/family-chore-chart`
- `/roommate-chore-planner`
- `/couple-chore-planner`
- `/household-workload-calculator`

Each page should have a distinct intent and an immediate interactive workflow.

## Programmatic SEO caution

Do not create hundreds of near-identical pages purely to capture keyword variations. Each indexable page should have unique functionality, examples or a distinct household use case.

## Acquisition channels

### Organic search

Primary channel.

### Sharing

A generated plan should be shareable with household members.

### Community distribution

Relevant communities could be tested carefully, but do not spam or manufacture endorsements.

### Content

Focus on genuinely useful interactive tools:

- chore load calculator
- household capacity calculator
- family chore chart generator
- roommate chore rotation generator

## SEO product loop

Search → instant tool → first result → save/share → account when useful → repeat planning.
