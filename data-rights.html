Data Rights & Export

Understand and exercise your data access, portability, and deletion rights.

1. Summary of Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access & Portability

Download a complete copy of your household configurations, members, tasks, and plans in standardized JSON format.

Right to Rectification

Modify or correct any household member details, available hours, or chore parameters directly inside the optimizer.

Right to Erasure (Deletion)

Permanently delete all your household records, members, plans, and account credentials with immediate effect.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Revoke consent for optional preference storage at any time via cookie preferences.

2. Direct Data Portability Export

If you are signed in, you can download a complete structured JSON archive of all your saved household data right now.

3. How to Request Assistance

If you cannot access your account or need manual verification, please contact our support team at support@chorematrix.com. We respond to valid data subject requests within 30 days without charge.
