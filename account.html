Session Management & Security

Explicit session termination scopes

Control which devices can access your household data. You can log out from this browser only, revoke access on other devices, or terminate all sessions worldwide.

Sign Out This Device

Terminates your session on this browser only. Other devices (such as your phone or work computer) remain signed in.

Sign Out All Other Devices

Revokes access on all other computers and phones while keeping this active browser session authenticated.

Sign Out Everywhere

Invalidates all refresh tokens globally across all devices, including this browser.

Danger Zone

Permanently delete your account and all associated household configurations, member details, and plans.
