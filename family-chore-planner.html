Why Traditional Family Chore Charts Get Abandoned

Sticker charts and magnetic chore boards treat every task the same. But cooking family dinners is fundamentally different from feeding the pet or making a bed. Without capacity limits and clear age eligibility, parents end up supervising every step or giving up after a few days.

❌ The Overwhelmed Parent Trap

Parents assign static chores, but sports practices, exam weeks, or late work meetings disrupt the schedule. Chores fall back entirely on one parent, leading to fatigue and household stress.

✓ Capacity & Role-Based Balancing

Set realistic weekly commitments for each family member (e.g., 60 mins for a child, 120 mins for a teen, 5 hours for parents). The optimizer assigns only eligible tasks and balances the load.

How ChoreMatrix Works for Families

1. Age-Appropriate Task Eligibility

Configure who can do what. Restrict stove cooking or deep bathroom chemicals to adults/teens, while opening trash, floor pickup, and pet care to children.

2. Proportional Capacity Modeling

Every family member has distinct available hours. ChoreMatrix scales chore assignments proportionally to each person's available capacity rather than expecting children to match adults.

3. Transparent Fairness Index

Children and teens see that the plan is calculated mathematically based on their free time and capacity, reducing complaints of favoritism or unfairness.
