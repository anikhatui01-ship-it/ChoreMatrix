1. Chore Optimizer vs Chore Tracker Approach

Instead of expecting partners or roommates to manually negotiate every chore, ChoreMatrix optimizes workload based on availability and effort weighting for fair chore division.

2. Capacity-Aware Balancing

Treats unequal capacity proportionally. An adult with 300 available minutes and a student with 120 minutes each shoulder fair proportional burdens.

3. Household Structure Flexibility

Designed for reality: couples, a family chore workload balancer for households with children, roommate chore division for flatmates, and multigenerational homes.

4. Hard Constraints vs Soft Preferences

Respects hard limits: strict task ineligibility and unavailable days are never violated. Soft preferences and aversions guide fair scoring.

5. Explainable Decision Tags

Every assignment includes transparent reasoning codes: eligible_match, capacity_relief, preference_bonus.

6. Automated Chore Rebalancing

When unexpected schedule changes happen, automated chore rebalancing recalculates settled tasks with minimal disruption.

7. Browser-First Spacious Experience

Spacious two-column workspace makes household members, capacities, task constraints, and schedule matrices visible together.

8. Deterministic Engine (Zero AI Hallucination)

Mathematically reproducible results in milliseconds. The exact same inputs always produce the exact same plan without LLM drift.

9. Multi-Week Rotational Fairness

Rotates demanding recurring tasks (such as deep bathroom scrubbing) across a 4-week horizon so no single member carries permanent burdens.

10. Explicit Overload Diagnostics

When required work exceeds total household capacity, ChoreMatrix flags the exact minute deficit rather than failing silently.
